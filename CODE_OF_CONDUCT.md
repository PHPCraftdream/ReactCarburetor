# Code of Conduct

React Carburetor welcomes people who want to use, improve, or question the
project. Discussions should stay focused on the work and treat participants
with respect, regardless of experience, identity, or background.

Be constructive in issues and pull requests: describe the behavior you saw,
share evidence, assume good faith, and accept technical disagreement without
personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and
unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable in project spaces.

This applies to the repository, its issue and pull-request discussions, and
other spaces presented as official project spaces. Maintainers may edit or
remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed to keep those
spaces safe.

To report a conduct concern privately, email **phpcraftdream@gmail.com** with
the subject `React Carburetor conduct`. Include links or context that help us
understand what happened. We will handle reports discreetly and avoid sharing
reporter details beyond what is needed to assess and respond to the concern.
