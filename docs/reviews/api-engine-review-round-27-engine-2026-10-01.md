## R27-ENG-01 — unrelated native payload accessor blocks request startup and locked-entry removal (P2)

**Supported contract and source.** `README.md:472-505,520-569` promises loading, refreshing, cache removal and eviction; `:656-661` promises restarts after readonly replay and actual removal of locked dictionary slots. `:902-910` defines native `Map` as an opaque supported runtime leaf, and `:668-671` limits the stricter accessor-native rejection to **history ownership**. State is plain enumerable data at its trackable levels (`:913-921`); the examples below have complete ordinary resource/cache state and own enumerable readonly fields, no accessors in a tracked plain container. Native `Map` metadata is not touched by their loaders or their removal action.

At `lib/src/Carburetor/Resource/prepareSlotRequest.ts:13-31` and `Resource/Cache/State/prepareCacheRequest.ts:17-47`, a restrictive **lifecycle field** triggers a full `cloneOwnedGraph` of the entire live graph *before* a loader can start. At `Resource/Cache/State/Mutation/omitLockedCacheEntries.ts:9-18`, a non-configurable dictionary slot likewise triggers a full copy even when the native payload belongs to an **unrelated, surviving** entry. `Store/Utils/Graph/cloneOwnedGraph.ts:29-89` recursively traverses native Map/Set/Date own descriptors and throws on an accessor at `:64-66`, with the *history-only* message `CarburetorHistory: cannot snapshot accessor property metadata`. The operational callers request `opaqueByReference=true`, but that exception applies only to other class instances (`:56-59`), not ordinary native objects. `ResourceCacheLifecycle.ts:381-407,295-316` makes this precondition observable from public `refresh` and `forget`.

**Repair direction.** Separate operational root/entry capability replacement from strict history ownership. Make the necessary writable lifecycle/dictionary endpoints without demanding history-grade traversal of unrelated opaque runtime payloads; preserve normal native identity/backlinks and descriptors where cloning is actually required. Do not call an accessor, erase native aliases, relax the readonly *held* history endpoint, or make ordinary draft writes to readonly fields succeed. The complete graph's strict accessor rejection remains correct for an actual history capture.
